    Before you print

    1. Paper size in the print dialog must match the paper here, for example A4.
    2. Scale must be 100% or “Actual size”. Never “Fit to page”.
    3. In the Epson dialog, turn off “Reduce/Enlarge” and “Fit to page”.
    4. Not sure it printed right? Press Print a scale test, then measure the 100 mm square.
