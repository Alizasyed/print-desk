Alignment test

Prints just five small crosses per side on one sheet, so use scrap or plain paper. Print the front, turn the sheet over the same way you will for the real job, then print the back on the other side. Hold it up to the light.

Look at the back side, upright. The front crosses show through. If the back crosses sit 1 mm to the right of them, type -1 in “Move the back to the right”. If they sit 1 mm too high, type -1 in “Move the back up”. Then print the test again.
